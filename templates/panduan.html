Section 2: Core In-Silico Scientific Equations & GEE Formulations

7 Deterministic Models
1. Radiation Attenuation Monsi-Saeki (1953)

Beer-Lambert Canopy Transmission

I = I₀ · exp(-k · LAI)

Where I₀ ≈ 1,800 µmol/m²/s is incident equatorial solar PAR, k = 0.45 is the calibrated oil palm extinction coefficient, and LAI(t) = 5.5 / [1 + exp(-0.7·(t - 3.5))] governs stand maturity. Leaving aside the 2.0m sterile circle, net corridor width is exactly 3.79m.

2. Land Sparing Efficiency Mead & Willey (1980)

Land Equivalent Ratio (LER) & PLS

LER = (Y_palm / M_palm) + (Y_crop / M_crop)
PLS (%) = [ 1.0 - (1.0 / LER) ] × 100.0

Quantifies biological land efficiency. LER > 1.0 confirms spatial sparing: polyculture yields equivalent output requiring less cumulative land. Fresh Fruit Bunch (FFB) yield deficit is capped at ≤ 4.5% to safeguard main cash crop viability.

3. High-Integrity Carbon Verra VM0007 / IPCC

Avoided Deforestation & Carbon Stock

Spared Ha = Area × (LER - 1.0)
tCO₂e = [ Spared × 450.0 ] + [ Area × 1.8 ]

Converts land sparing gains into primary rainforest area protected from agricultural expansion. Uses benchmark tropical lowland emission factor of 450.0 tCO₂e/Ha (Verra VM0007) plus annual soil organic carbon (SOC) sequestration of 1.8 tCO₂e/Ha/Yr.

4. GEE Satellite Reflectance Copernicus Sentinel-2

Sentinel-2 NDVI & NDMI Metrics

NDVI = (B8 - B4) / (B8 + B4)
NDMI = (B8 - B11) / (B8 + B11)

Extracted via Google Earth Engine API (cloud filter < 20%). Calibrates empirical canopy vigor (NDVI ≥ 0.75 target) and root zone moisture content (NDMI ≥ 0.35) prior to running in-silico agroforestry simulations.

5. Eco-Hydrology Sponge USDA-NRCS SCS-CN

Aquifer Recharge & Runoff Suppression

Q = (P - Iₐ)² / (P - Iₐ + S)
Recharge = P · [1 - CN/100] · 10 · 1.25

Multilayer canopy interception and contour silt pits (rorak) decrease runoff Curve Number from 82 to 64 (-22.0%), suppressing topsoil erosion by -68.4% and recharging local aquifers by +1,850 m³/Ha/year.

6. Nutrient Stoichiometry PPKS Agronomy

NPK Balance & Frond Bio-Circularity

Urea Saved = N_bio · (100 / 46)
MOP Supp = K₂O_need · (100 / 60)

Legume companion biological N-fixation offsets up to 141.3 kg Urea/Ha. Potassium-demanding rhizomes receive compensatory +75 kg MOP/Ha in active alleys. Shredded pruned fronds return +36.2 kg organic K₂O/Ha.

Section 6: Technical Q&A & Troubleshooting (FAQ)

Technical FAQ
❓

Why is the 2.0-meter oil palm weed circle strictly prohibited from intercrops and mechanical hoeing?

The 2.0m radius around palm bases contains the dense concentration of active superficial feeder roots responsible for absorbing over 80% of applied baseline fertilizers. Planting within this circle causes direct resource competition. Furthermore, mechanical hoeing injuries on root tissues serve as primary entry points for fatal soil-borne pathogens like basal stem rot (Ganoderma boninense).

❓

How does Google Earth Engine verify EUDR post-2020 deforestation compliance?

The backend queries the Hansen Global Forest Change dataset (UMD/hansen/global_forest_change) over the concession geometry. Pixels flagging tree cover loss during years 2021 through present are quantified. If tree loss is exactly 0.00 Ha, the estate is awarded verified EUDR Article 9 compliance.

❓

Why does the mobile field app use IndexedDB instead of raw SQLite files on smartphones?

The mobile app operates as a Progressive Web App (PWA) within a sandboxed mobile browser environment that restricts direct raw file system writes. IndexedDB is the official high-performance transactional database on mobile devices, capable of storing high-resolution photos and GPS telemetry permanently offline, synchronizing seamlessly to the central server upon reconnecting.

❓

What is the 'All Report' document and how does the SHA-256 hash prove data authenticity?

The All-Report document in Stage 4 (Roadmap) consolidates the complete 4-stage lifecycle: Stage 1 (Land Title), Stage 2A (PIPPIB Moratorium & GEE Satellite), Stage 2B (In-Silico LER), and Stage 3 (Multi-Photo Drone Logs). A 64-character SHA-256 cryptographic digest locks all variables; any alteration of plot coordinates or yields immediately fails hash verification upon scanning at /validasi.
